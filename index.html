An afternoon of proven practice from people who do the work

The Tri-Cities Quality Forum is a regional conference with a national audience, dedicated to quality management, performance excellence, and continuous improvement in every sector.

For 67 years, we've gathered statisticians, quality executives, continuous improvement specialists, and founders to share real case studies and practical tools. Whether you work in manufacturing, healthcare, services, or technology, you'll leave with lessons you can apply right away to remove waste and raise performance.

What the sessions cover

The program each year draws from three disciplines that drive modern quality and operational success.

Quality technologies

Statistical process control, design of experiments, data analytics, and modeling for autocorrelated processes.

Quality 4.0 and AI

Applying artificial intelligence, computer vision, large language models, and automated analytics to modern quality frameworks.

Leadership and improvement

Building leadership systems, leading multi-generational and remote teams, and putting Baldrige methods to work.
