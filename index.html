About the Forum

The Tri-Cities Quality Forum (TCQF) is a premier regional and national conference dedicated to advancing the practices of quality management, performance excellence, and continuous improvement across various sectors.

For 66 years, we've gathered leading statisticians, quality executives, continuous improvement specialists, and founders to share their insights, real-world case studies, and practical tools. Whether you're in manufacturing, healthcare, service industries, or technology, the forum offers actionable lessons that you can apply immediately to remove waste and elevate performance.
